# Explainability

## Decision and Reasoning

The Job Market Research Agent makes deterministic decisions by applying predefined mathematical and rule-based operations to structured job-market data. It determines results by validating the supplied data and applying the specific calculation or aggregation required by the selected analysis operation.

## Inputs and Data Used

The agent uses structured job-market data supplied to its tools, including job listings, skills, salary information, locations, companies, and candidate or job skill sets where applicable. The calculations use only the supplied input data and do not require hidden information or probabilistic model inference.

## Limits and Known Constraints

The agent is limited to the data supplied by the user and does not independently retrieve live job-market information or guarantee future hiring outcomes. Its results are deterministic summaries of the provided dataset and cannot establish facts that are absent from that dataset.
